That Strange Fact

The strange side of history. Curiosity Leads Further.

That Strange Fact is a history and curiosity channel on YouTube and Instagram. Each video takes a strange tradition, object, place or documented mystery and asks a real question about it. The answers are researched from real sources, and folklore is always told as folklore, never presented as fact.

About the ThatSpookyFact Production app

ThatSpookyFact Production is a private tool used only by the channel's owner to run the channel. It is not offered to the public and does not ask anyone else to sign in. With the owner's permission it:

  • reads and writes the channel's own production files in the owner's Google Drive, and delivers finished videos there for review;
  • uploads the channel's own finished Shorts, once a weekday, to the owner's own YouTube channel and Instagram account;
  • reads the channel's own YouTube Analytics (read-only) to produce a weekly performance report.

It uploads only the channel's own original videos. It never edits or deletes anything on YouTube or Instagram, and never touches any other channel or account.

How the app handles account data is described in the privacy policy; its use is covered by the terms of service.
